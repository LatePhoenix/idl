## Deviations

- Framing is applied when the base is a vector picture. A procedural base keeps the full 1024 square, so a vector part on the placeholder head stays where it was. Those bases are retired in AP-7.
- The numbers in §3.3 were used as written. They were not tuned. Bust makes the face about 20% smaller (`1024/1280`). Head makes it about 7% smaller (`1024/1104`).
