## Deviations

- The spec sketches the crew collar at y ≈ 960–1010 (50 units). At head scale 48/1104 that band is about 2.2 px, short of the 3-row acceptance. The collar is y 948–1024 (76 units) so the test passes. Shoulders still read at 512 px.
- A 240-wide neck cannot also sit 20 units inside the face at y 860, where the face is about 251 wide. The silhouette test checks that the neck starts at y ≤ 860, that the chin covers it by at least 20 (912 − 840 = 72), that the width stays ≤ 240 through y 1000, and that the shoulders reach x ≤ −100 and x ≥ 1100 at y 1200.
- Dark contact sheets put the tiles on `#17131B`. The avatar's own scene is still the light `scene_plain` gradient. Wallpaper outline contrast is AP-9.
- `ap1_compare_*.png` draws the base picture in the 1024 square, with no framing and no shirt. The brand panel shows the neck and clips the shoulders, which sit below y 1024. The shirt appears on resolved avatars.
