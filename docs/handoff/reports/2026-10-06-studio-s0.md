- The preview skips procedural layers, the outline-contrast override and procedural overlays such as
  `overlay_blush`. The emoji pack's happy faces list `overlay_blush`, which is a `core_proto` procedural
  asset, while `overlay_cheek_blush` (vector) isn't referenced by any expression. Worth checking in the
  next audit.
- AP-8 cross-asset masks (`publishMask`/`clipBy`) are previewed best effort until S2 uses the real resolver.
- The page reads files on each Reload; it doesn't watch the disk.
